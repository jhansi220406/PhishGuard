Transparent Process

Three-Step Inspection Architecture

How PhishGuard assesses links and payloads safely without compromising your endpoint.

01

Submit or Scan

Provide a raw URL string or scan a physical QR code. Payloads are sanitized and parsed without opening the target web page.

02

Deterministic Analysis

The engine tests across 12+ risk dimensions: IP hostname, suspicious TLD, typosquatting, credential traps, redirect keywords, and TLS context.

03

Explainable Verdict

Receive an explainable risk score (0-100), full itemized findings with severity levels, and concrete recommended defense measures.

Cybersecurity Assessment Notice & Limitations

PhishGuard provides deterministic risk indicators and heuristic security analysis. Findings represent evaluated threat probability and do not constitute an absolute guarantee that any site is safe or malicious. Always exercise caution and never submit sensitive credentials on unfamiliar pages.
