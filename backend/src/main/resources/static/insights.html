Threat Anatomy & Breakdown

CRITICAL Lookalike Domains

Attackers register domains visually indistinguishable from brands like Google or PayPal using Unicode homoglyphs (e.g. Cyrillic 'а') or subtle typos.

HIGH Raw IP Hostnames

Phishers bypass DNS blacklisting and domain registration records by hosting credential harvester kits on ephemeral raw IPv4 or IPv6 web servers.

MEDIUM High-Risk TLDs

Free or cheap Top-Level Domains (.tk, .xyz, .top, .ml, .work) account for a disproportionate volume of short-lived disposable phishing attacks.

Actionable Defensive Measures for Organizations & End Users

  • Implement FIDO2 / WebAuthn Hardware Keys: Phishing-resistant MFA stops credential theft even if users mistakenly visit lookalike sites.
  • Treat Physical QR Codes as Untrusted: Attackers tamper with parking meters, restaurant menus, and payment signs with malicious stickers.
  • Audit DNS with Protective Filtering: Block resolution of newly registered domains (NRDs < 24h old) to prevent day-zero campaigns.
  • Educate Users on the HTTPS Misconception: Ensure staff know that a padlock only guarantees encryption with the remote server, not identity honesty.
